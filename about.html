ADFX Sound / Andrew Dearing is a sound designer with experience developing a wide arranging styles of video games. Having spent the past 18 years developing with relatively small audio teams, his skill set is rounded out with equal parts design and technical. Particularly well versed prototyping in Unreal Engine using Blueprint integrated with AudioKinetic Wwise.

  • 18 Years Game Development w/ UE5, UE4, UE3, Unity, Proprietary Engines

  • Shipped 11+ games using Audiokinetic Wwise

  • Reaper, Pro Tools (Assets / Cinematics)

  • Field Recording (8040ST, various handhelds etc)

  • Custom Sound Effects Libraries, w/ UCS Metadata

  • Expertise Building Prototypes with Unreal Blueprint and more recently c++

  • Dedicated 5.1 Genelec Equipped

  • VPN/Remote P4 Accessible on a Fiber Line
